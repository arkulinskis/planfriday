How it works

From "we should have people over" to a full table, before the weekend starts.

Two minutes

Order from the catalogue

Pick a party idea (Soup Night, Taco Saturday, Pancake Brunch...) or start blank. Tick the weekend slots that could work.

Friday afternoon

Send one link

Text it, WhatsApp it, email it. Friends open it, put their name down, tick the days they're free and pick a dish.

Friday night

Lock in the day

The best day rises to the top. Lock it in, everyone adds it to their calendar, and the menu fills itself.

For the host: you make the page

  • A party page with your picture, your note, your address
  • Slots across Friday, Saturday and Sunday (this weekend or next)
  • A menu by section (soup, mains, dessert, drinks...) with how many of each you want
  • Lock in the best day, close RSVPs, set a seat limit
  • A weather check for every slot, a printable order sheet, and "run it again" for next week

For the guests: got a link from a friend?

  • Open the link. No app, no account, no password
  • Put your name down and RSVP, with a +1 or two
  • Tick ✓ free, ~ maybe or ✕ can't for each day
  • Say what you're bringing, or let the dice suggest something
  • Note your diet, leave a note for the host, add the day to your calendar

Questions & answers

Do my guests need an account?

No. They open your link, type their name, and they're on the list. Their browser remembers them. Only the host needs an account (free), so every party has an owner.

Can it really be last minute?

That's the whole idea. Make the party on Friday morning, send the link at lunch, lock in Saturday dinner by the evening. Your friends see changes right away.

Where does the weather come from?

From Open-Meteo, a free weather service. Our server asks it for the forecast in the town the host typed, so your browser never contacts it, and the forecast is refreshed every hour.

What if nobody is free?

Edit the party and add more slots, or tick next weekend too. Answers people already gave are kept.

Who can see my party?

Anyone with the link. It's long and random, so nobody finds it by chance. Guests see first names, RSVPs, dishes and notes, never emails. You can close RSVPs, remove someone, or delete the party at any time.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no fees, nothing to install. It works in any browser, and you can add it to your phone's home screen.
